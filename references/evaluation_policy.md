# Evaluation Policy

Historical replay is for finding obvious logic and execution-model problems.

Forward paper trading is the primary evidence before any live mode is considered. Performance must include:

- Net P/L.
- Realized P/L.
- Unrealized P/L.
- Win rate.
- Average win/loss.
- Max drawdown.
- Trade count.
- Fill rate.
- Rejections and open orders.

Profitability claims must include the quote source, timestamps, costs, slippage, and unfilled order count.
