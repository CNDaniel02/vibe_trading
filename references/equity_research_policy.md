# Equity Research Policy

Limit first-version research to liquid US stocks and ordinary non-levered ETFs.

Every candidate must include:

- Symbol.
- Observation timestamp.
- Quote timestamp.
- Source name.
- Thesis text.
- Entry plan.
- Exit plan.

Reject candidates with missing quote data, stale quote data, abnormal spreads, OTC listings, leveraged/inverse ETFs, or unsupported asset classes.
